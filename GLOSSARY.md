# GetMath

The language of the GetMath book and how it is made.

## Language

### Structure

**Part**:
A group of chapters on one subject, in reading order.
_Avoid_: Unit, module, volume

**Chapter**:
The unit that is specified, reviewed, tested by Ammar and accepted. It has one main outcome and usually 3 to 6 lessons.
_Avoid_: Unit, module

**Lesson**:
One page that teaches one idea in one sitting, about 45 to 60 minutes. It has no approval of its own.
_Avoid_: Section, page

**Topic**:
A subject listed in the gap assessment. A lesson may cover several topics, and a topic may span several lessons.
_Avoid_: Gap (a gap is a topic that MML assumes, leaves out or does not develop enough)

**Capstone**:
The last chapter of Parts I, II and III. It fits a line with that part's mathematics and reviews the part.
_Avoid_: Part review, final project

**Optional lesson**:
A lesson that no required lesson uses and no hidden test covers.
_Avoid_: Enrichment, extra, bonus

**Milestone**:
A delivery of the book: Ready to Start ML, then Full Book.
_Avoid_: Phase, release

### Testing

**Pilot**:
The first chapter built, on vectors, to test the method. It is later deleted and its subject is written again in reading order.
_Avoid_: Sample chapter, draft chapter

**Hidden test**:
Transfer problems written by the independent reviewer that Ammar does not see before the test.
_Avoid_: Quiz, exam

**Retrieval check**:
A short test of an accepted chapter, taken at least two weeks after its hidden test.
_Avoid_: Review, revision

**Readiness check**:
A short set of questions that lets a learner skip foundations they already know.
_Avoid_: Placement test, pre-test

**Returning dataset**:
The dataset that recurs through the book, so that the learner sees the same data studied in new ways.
_Avoid_: Running example
